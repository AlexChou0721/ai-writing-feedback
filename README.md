# AI Writing Feedback Assistant

A teacher-oriented AI-assisted writing feedback tool designed for English language learning.

This project explores how generative AI can support English writing education by providing structured, pedagogically meaningful feedback, rather than automated correction or scoring.


## Background & Motivation

My academic background is in English studies, and I have hands-on experience in English language teaching. Through teaching and tutoring, I observed that students often struggle not only with grammar, but more importantly with unnatural expressions, vague logic, and non-native phrasing.

Traditional feedback is time-consuming and difficult to scale, while many AI writing tools focus on surface-level correction or produce full rewrites that discourage genuine learning.

This project was initiated to explore whether generative AI can be used as a supportive tool in writing education — one that helps learners reflect on their language use, while preserving the teacher’s pedagogical role and judgment.


## What the Tool Does

The AI Writing Feedback Assistant is designed to provide structured feedback on student writing, focusing on learning-oriented improvement rather than automatic rewriting.

Given a short English text, the tool returns feedback in three main parts:

1. **Unnatural expressions**  
   Identification of phrases or sentences that sound non-native or awkward, with brief explanations.

2. **Suggested revisions**  
   Improved versions that preserve the original meaning while demonstrating more natural usage.

3. **Teaching notes**  
   Short pedagogical comments that highlight common language patterns, collocation issues, or logic problems, helping learners generalize beyond a single example.

The current version uses a demo setup to illustrate the feedback structure and user interaction.


## Design Philosophy (Teacher-oriented)

This project is intentionally designed from a teacher’s perspective.

Instead of producing a fully rewritten essay or assigning scores, the tool prioritizes explanation, comparison, and reflection. The goal is not to replace teachers, but to support the feedback process by making high-quality, formative comments more accessible and scalable.

By keeping the feedback selective and explanatory, the tool encourages learners to actively engage with revisions, rather than passively accept AI-generated text.


## Limitations

As an exploratory educational project, the current version of the tool has several limitations.

First, generative AI may occasionally overgeneralize or produce feedback that lacks sufficient sensitivity to individual learner needs. Language learning is highly contextual, and no automated system can fully replace human judgment.

Second, the demo version does not yet incorporate real-time model interaction or personalized learning history, which limits its adaptability across different proficiency levels.

These limitations reinforce the importance of positioning AI as a supportive tool within education, rather than an authoritative evaluator.


## Future Work

Future development of this project may include:

- Integrating a live generative AI model through a secure backend to provide real-time feedback.
- Allowing teachers to customize feedback focus areas based on instructional goals.
- Exploring how learner interaction data can inform more adaptive and context-aware feedback.
- Further examining ethical considerations in AI-assisted language education, such as transparency and learner agency.

These directions align with my broader academic interest in the intersection of generative AI, language, and the humanities.
